Who we are

One team standing behind every shipment.

Eurasia Bridge is a sourcing and trade agent built around one corridor — Iran to Kazakhstan and the wider CIS. We connect the two sides of a deal and coordinate the parts that usually take four separate companies: finding and vetting the supplier, arranging transport by rail, ferry or road through vetted carriers, clearing customs on both sides, settling payment — including barter where a clean cash channel is hard — and getting your goods onto the shelf. You work with one team on a transparent commission, not a chain of intermediaries.

We are based in Kazakhstan and Iran and work in Russian, English and Persian, so both sides of the deal speak to one accountable partner instead of a chain of handoffs.
